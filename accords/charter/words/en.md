# Concordance Charter

*Concordance* (from the Latin for "hearts together") is a way for people to look after each other: sharing what we have, governing fairly what is scarce, and deciding together, in small circles anyone may join and anyone may leave.

Everything here is an attempt to practice love: to share, to respect each other's freedom, and to care for one another. We hope for a world where no one goes unnoticed, conflicts end in repair, and the best things in life are given rather than sold. It grows from the small places where we live.

## Our principles

1. **Open to all.** Anyone may join, and anyone may leave with their history. We are for each other, never against anyone. People of every faith and none are welcome, and we honor each other's beliefs without pressing our own.
2. **Local rules.** Those affected decide.
3. **Freely given.** What passes between us is given freely and received with thanks.
4. **Need first.** Scarce things go first to those who need them most.
5. **Our own.** Each of us holds our own information, and our network belongs to its members. What's shared in private stays private.
6. **Equals together.** Circles cooperate as equals.
7. **Held in trust.** Land and shared things are held for the community and never sold for private gain.

## How we live

We answer when asked. We notice who's missing. We stand by each other in illness and loss. We work, eat, rest and celebrate together. We speak kindly, disagree well and thank generously. We share the work and pass on what we know. When we no longer know each other by name, we seed a new circle.

## How we decide

We listen to everyone first. Small things go ahead unless someone has a concern; bigger things need agreement everyone can live with. If it's safe to try, we try it and learn. We think of those who come after us.

## When things go wrong

We repair harm rather than blame, gently and step by step. Disagreements are heard by people both sides trust. Safety comes first: concerns about a child or vulnerable adult go straight to the person we've chosen for safety, and to the authorities where the law requires.

## Joining and changing

To join, introduce yourself, and a member who has met you welcomes you in. Offer one thing you can give, and sign. Our tools are open and our own, and anyone can take part without them. We revisit this charter together; anyone who can't accept a change may leave or begin a new circle.
