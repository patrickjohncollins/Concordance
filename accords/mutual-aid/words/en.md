# Mutual Aid

We help each other through hard times. Anyone can ask for help: a meal, a lift, an errand, company. Someone answers soon, even if the answer is no. Urgent needs go to the whole circle. Anyone who wants us to check on them can join our check-on-me list, seen only by the people who coordinate it. When several needs compete, the greatest need comes first. We thank each other, and no one keeps score.
