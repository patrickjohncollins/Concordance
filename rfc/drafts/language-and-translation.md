Most of the world's cooperative traditions live in languages technology serves poorly. Concordance treats every language as equal.

**The charter**

- **Translated and adapted by people** from circles that will live by it; machine translation may draft, people decide. Translators may use local images and words (*dugnad*, *minga*, *harambee*) where they say it better.
- **Every language version has equal standing.** There is no English master copy: each translation is a signed record pointing to the charter version it translates, and drift in meaning is resolved by proposal like any other change.
- **A translation commons** holds translations of the charter, accords, stories and the app interface, open to every new circle.

**Circles across languages**

- **Translation on the hearth:** messages, needs and offers translated by a local model, so nothing private leaves the circle.
- **The original is always one tap away,** so translation never puts words in anyone's mouth.
- **Bridge members:** bilingual members take a recognized role, especially in decisions.
- **Decisions slow down:** proposals appear in every member's language, and listening takes longer on purpose. The charter, safety messages and decisions are always checked by a person.

**Beyond written words**

- **Voice:** needs, offers and introductions can be spoken and played back; the charter can be heard as well as read.
- **Symbols:** simple icons for common needs (a meal, a lift, a repair, a visit).
- **Sign languages:** video introductions and the charter in sign languages, made by deaf members.
- **All scripts from v0:** right-to-left, complex and minority-language scripts supported from the start.

**Selewa, Concordance's own language.** Alongside every mother tongue, Concordance grows an auxiliary language of its own. *Selewa* ("hearts together") is Concordance's name for itself in that language; in English text we say Concordance, and write Selewa only for the language itself. The seed is in `accords/selewa/`, and its design is in the `selewa-meaning-layer` draft.
